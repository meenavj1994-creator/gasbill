Upload your REG-06 to fill the fields below, or skip and type them yourself.

Business identity

This is what prints on every tax invoice.

3
Invoice numbering

Rule 46(b) caps the invoice number at 16 characters.

If another machine at this agency already runs GasBill, give this one a different prefix — say SB where the other uses SH. Each machine counts on its own, so a shared prefix means both issue the same invoice number.

4
Customer acknowledgement

Printed on the distributor copy only, above the customer's signature.

Have your CA check this wording. It is what the customer signs against.

5
Backup folder

Optional, but strongly recommended — this is your only offsite copy.

Pick a folder inside Google Drive or OneDrive and backups sync offsite automatically.
