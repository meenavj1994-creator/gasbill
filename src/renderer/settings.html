Business details

—

These are the details that print on every invoice. Changing them affects invoices raised from now on — invoices already saved keep the details they were raised with.

1
Business identity

Name, address and GSTIN as they appear on the invoice.

2
Invoice numbering

Rule 46(b) caps the invoice number at 16 characters.

Changing this does not renumber invoices already saved, and the monthly counter keeps running from where it is.

Every machine at this agency needs its own prefix. Counters are per machine, so two machines sharing a prefix will issue the same invoice number to different customers.

3
Invoice wording

The acknowledgement prints on the distributor copy only, above the customer's signature. The other two lines print on both copies.

Have your CA check this wording. It is what the customer signs against.

Leave blank to leave it off.

Optional. Prints small at the foot of every copy.

4
Backup folder

A copy of the database is written here every time the app closes.
